Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

Access and use

Simba Google Integration uses data from Google services, within the permissions granted by the user, to perform requested tasks and approved workflows. These workflows may access data and carry out authorized actions automatically.

Processing and storage

Selected data may be shared with configured AI providers for processing and with Telegram for private notifications. Provider terms and settings apply. Credentials and tokens are stored on the operator-controlled server, not on this website. Selected data may remain in conversation history, working files and backups; no single automatic deletion period applies.

Limited Use

The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising targeting, or use it to train generalized AI models. Use and transfer of Google API data will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

You can revoke Google access at any time. This stops future access under that authorization but does not automatically erase stored data or backups. Authorized users can contact the operator through their existing private communication channel for privacy or deletion requests, subject to provider and backup limitations.

This website

No sign-in, forms, analytics or advertising scripts are used. The hosting provider may process technical request information to operate the site.

Private personal-use integration. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
